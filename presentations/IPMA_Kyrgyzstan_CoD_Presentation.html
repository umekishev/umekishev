Strategic position of Kyrgyzstan

A Central Asian gateway for professional project management

Area199,900 km² — mountainous heart of Central Asia
Population~7.2 million (2024)
Capital / HQBishkek — proposed head office of the MA
Key sectorsEnergy & hydropower, mining, infrastructure, agriculture, IT & digital services, public investment programmes
Regional roleBridge between Central Asia, corridor projects and Eurasian markets
OpportunityGrowing demand for competent P3M capability in national and donor-funded projects

Our Mission

Promote and strengthen professionalism in project, programme and portfolio management across Kyrgyzstan — so that public and private projects achieve their objectives efficiently, ethically and in line with IPMA values and standards.

A national home for the IPMA ecosystem in Central Asia.

IPMA Kyrgyzstan

Strategic goals

Clear priorities for the first three years

1

Establish IPMA Kyrgyzstan as a recognised professional body in the Kyrgyz Republic.

2

Grow a stable community of individual and corporate members from a clear baseline.

3

Establish the Kyrgyzstan Certification Body across all 4 IPMA levels.

4

Achieve sustained certification growth and quality of assessment.

5

Promote the IPMA standard across Kyrgyzstan and the wider Central Asian region.

6

Launch and develop Young Crew Kyrgyzstan with universities.

7

Participate in and organise IPMA events, webinars and knowledge exchanges.

8

Build durable partnerships with training providers, employers and peer MAs.

IPMA Kyrgyzstan

Co-creating our Certification Body

Quality certification rooted in collaboration with peer MAs / CBs

  • Offer all 4 IPMA certification levels, with a phased start where needed
  • Collaborate with established MAs / CBs to harmonise process and quality
  • Build a committed pool of assessors and share capacity regionally where appropriate
  • Outsource examination processing where it strengthens reliability and speed
  • Protect candidate experience and the reputation of the IPMA brand
  • Reinvest certification income into community development and Young Crew
